# Dominican Republic and Colombia: comparable home-service platforms

Research date: November 17, 2026, America/New_York.

Deliverable: [Excel comparison](DR-Colombia-Home-Services-Comparison-2026-11-17.xlsx). It contains 10 main company comparisons, six additional watchlist candidates, 23 pricing records and 33 source records. Filters, frozen identifier columns, native-currency numeric pricing and clickable source links are included. Underlying research is in the companion JSON; build script is build-market-comparison.py (requires openpyxl).

Comparable offerings exist in both markets. The useful distinctions are what triggers payment, who pays and how much work the platform performs after introducing the parties.

- **Dominican Republic:** [Servicio.do](https://servicio.do/pricing) publishes provider subscriptions and a 10% completed-job fee capped at USD 100. [MANO](https://appmano.com/) advertises credits for opportunities and a Santiago waitlist. [Reno RD](https://renord.do/legal/proveedores) describes category commissions and some subscriptions. SuperVecino targets residential communities; Corotos is an adjacent classifieds/directory substitute. Servi at servird.do explicitly presents a Santo Domingo prelaunch offer.
- **Colombia:** [Solverio](https://solverio.co/tarifas) publishes COP 1,500–2,500 per submitted proposal, with an additional 3% winner fee for companies and no job commission for individuals. [AYDA](https://ayda.com.co/preguntas-frecuentes) describes a completed-service percentage without disclosing its rate. Hogarú and Homecenter are more operationally involved comparators; their retail service prices are different from professional lead fees.

The detailed workbook distinguishes publicly accessible offers, pilot/waitlist pages and uncertain current operation. It does not establish market share, transaction volume, successful fulfillment or profitability. Provider verification claims are self-reported, not independently audited. No company was contacted and no booking was made.

Important interpretation checks:

- AYDA's [electrician entry offer](https://ayda.com.co/servicios/reparacion-mantenimiento/electricista) is a quotation visit, not a complete repair price.
- Homecenter's [four-hour handyman SKU](https://www.homecenter.com.co/homecenter-co/product/310473/maestro-por-medio-dia-4-horas/310473/) is a retail labour package; materials and other scope conditions matter.
- Servicio.do's current web pricing and its linked older Punta Cana Work app description differ. Its professional page and detailed pricing page also describe different plan counts. Confirm the current professional agreement.
- Habitissimo Colombia could not be confirmed; a failed fetch is not evidence of closure. Timbrit's retrieved local page contains old examples and current provider pricing could not be checked. Jelpit's current homepage emphasizes collections/payments; historical home-service material does not prove current booking availability. These belong on the watchlist.
- servird.do and servi.vrd.do are different observed domains and are not assumed to be one business. The latter shows zero businesses/cities alongside marketing claims.

MEA recommendation, as analysis: begin with a narrow Santo Domingo service area and dependable providers, measure qualified demand and actual job wins, then choose a simple charge event. Paid proposals, released contacts and completed-job commissions allocate risk differently. Do not import Colombian amounts as Dominican willingness-to-pay data. This report does not approve a tariff, payment provider, business-model change or launch date.

Artifact checks: Excel package integrity, worksheet/record counts, source-reference validity, numeric percentage/currency columns and hyperlink structure were checked programmatically. No application code, database, infrastructure or deployment was changed.
